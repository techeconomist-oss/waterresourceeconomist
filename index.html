01 — About

Research that reaches the field and the policy table.

Dr. D. Suresh Kumar is deeply committed to sustainable water resource management and agricultural development through evidence-based, policy-oriented research and dialogue. His expertise spans water resources, ecosystem services and their valuation, and the impact assessment of development programmes, with a strong record in publications, fund raising, research to impact, government relations, capacity building and policy engagement.

He has handled 55 research projects, 26 as Principal Investigator and 29 as Co-Investigator, with organisations such as IWMI, ICRISAT, the Global Development Network, SANDEE (Nepal), IFS (Sweden), the Institute of Developing Economies (Japan), Monash University (Malaysia), IFPRI, WRI, ICAR, DST, NABARD, INCID, the State Planning Commission and TAWDEVA, mobilising ₹4,847 lakh. His recommendations cover sustainable management of water resources, watershed management, collective action, productivity and guaranteed farm income through Payment for Ecosystem Services.

He developed WatDIMP, a computer-based model for assessing the impact of watershed development, and has trained officials who implement watershed programmes. He holds three copyrights, including WatDIMP, and facilitated a Geographical Indication for Kumbakonam betel vine. He has also helped assess 40 years of water management research in India to identify technologies for scaling up nationally.

As Nodal Officer of the TNAU–FPO Linkage (TFL) programme, he facilitated agreements between 112 Farmer Producer Organisations and units of TNAU, supporting market linkages, networking and business development. The programme helped FPOs raise their turnover by 14% and net income by 9%.

02 — Expertise

Where water, economics and institutions meet.

Six connected areas run through his research, consultancy and teaching.

Water Resource Economics & Policy

Pricing, allocation and market-based instruments for efficient irrigation water use, and the water–energy nexus in agriculture.

Tank Irrigation & Institutions

Tank cascades, storage performance and investment priorities for South India's traditional irrigation tanks under climate variability.

Micro Irrigation

Social cost–benefit analysis and adoption of drip irrigation, and its impact on farming systems and resource use.

Watershed Impact Assessment

Methods and models for evaluating watershed programmes, including PMKSY, IWMP and NABARD projects across Tamil Nadu.

Ecosystem Services Valuation

Theory and applied methods for valuing nature, from mangroves to organic farming, and their use in agricultural policy.

Climate Adaptation & Risk

Climate variability, drought proofing, crop insurance and adaptation in urbanising watersheds and irrigated ecosystems.

04 — Software
Copyright registered · 2025

WatDIMP

Watershed Development Impact Assessment Model

WatDIMP is an interactive, computer-based model for assessing the impact of watershed development programmes. It began as WADIAM and was rebuilt with support from the Department of Science and Technology, Government of India, adding interactive utilities, menus and options.

The model also carries reference material on watershed development concepts, programme guidelines and impact assessment methods.
